IT SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Category: Access Management
Document: Account, Password and Permission Support
Version: 1.0

PURPOSE
This document covers common account access, password, account lockout, and permission requests.

COMMON SYMPTOMS
- User cannot sign in.
- Account is locked.
- Password reset is required.
- Multi-factor authentication is not working.
- User can sign in but cannot access a folder or application.
- Access is requested for a new role or project.

INITIAL CHECKS
1. Confirm the application or service the user is trying to access.
2. Record the exact error message and time it occurred.
3. Check whether the user can sign in to other approved services.
4. Ask whether the user recently changed their password or enrolled a new MFA device.
5. For a permission request, confirm the business reason and the resource owner who can approve access.
6. Direct users to the organization's official password-reset or account-recovery portal.

LIKELY CAUSES
- Incorrect username or password.
- Account lockout after repeated failed attempts.
- Expired password or account.
- MFA device or enrollment issue.
- Missing group membership or role permission.
- Approval or provisioning not completed.

ESCALATION
Assign password, account lockout, and MFA problems to IT Helpdesk or the designated Identity and Access Management team. Route application-specific authorization issues to the relevant application owner. Requests for privileged access require the formal approval process.

SECURITY AND SAFETY
Never ask users to send passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, or MFA approval prompts to support staff. Do not bypass MFA or grant access based solely on a ticket. Access changes must follow identity verification, least privilege, and approval procedures.

RECOMMENDED TICKET DETAILS
- Username or approved employee identifier
- Application or resource name
- Exact error message
- Whether sign-in works elsewhere
- Whether password or MFA settings recently changed
- Business justification and resource owner approval for access requests
