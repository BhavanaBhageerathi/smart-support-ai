Category: Hardware
Document: Desktop, Laptop and Peripheral Troubleshooting
Version: 1.0

PURPOSE
This document covers basic checks for hardware faults involving computers and peripherals.

COMMON SYMPTOMS
- Laptop or desktop does not power on.
- Device becomes unusually hot or shuts down unexpectedly.
- Keyboard, mouse, monitor, webcam, or docking station is not detected.
- Printer or other peripheral does not respond.
- Screen is blank or displays unusual artifacts.

INITIAL CHECKS
1. Ask whether the device has physical damage, liquid exposure, or a burning smell.
2. Check that the power cable, adapter, dock, and monitor cables are firmly connected.
3. Confirm that the power outlet is working using an approved method.
4. For a blank monitor, check the monitor power, input source, brightness, and video cable.
5. For a keyboard or mouse, try another approved USB port or reconnect the wireless receiver.
6. Restart the device only if there are no signs of electrical or physical damage.

WHEN NOT TO CONTINUE
Stop troubleshooting and escalate immediately if there is:
- Smoke, sparks, burning smell, battery swelling, or liquid damage.
- A damaged power adapter or exposed wiring.
- Unusual clicking or grinding noises from a storage device when data may be at risk.

LIKELY CAUSES
- Loose or faulty cable or power adapter.
- Failed peripheral or port.
- Battery or charging problem.
- Display input or cable issue.
- Hardware component failure or overheating.

ESCALATION
Assign to Hardware Support when basic connection checks do not resolve the issue, a component appears faulty, or physical inspection or replacement is required.

SECURITY AND SAFETY
Users must not open device enclosures or attempt component replacement unless authorized. Do not recommend opening a power supply or swollen battery. Preserve the device and escalate safety-related symptoms immediately.

RECOMMENDED TICKET DETAILS
- Device make, model, and asset tag
- Peripheral involved
- Whether power indicators are visible
- Recent drops, spills, updates, or hardware changes
- Error lights, beep codes, or exact symptoms
