IT SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Category: Other
Document: General IT Requests and Triage Guidance
Version: 1.0

PURPOSE
This document helps triage requests that do not clearly fit network, hardware, software, database, security, access management, or email categories.

EXAMPLES
- Request for a new approved application.
- Request for a laptop, monitor, or other IT equipment.
- Request for general technical guidance.
- Request for a system configuration change.
- A ticket with insufficient information to identify the issue.

INITIAL TRIAGE
1. Identify what the requester is trying to achieve.
2. Determine whether the request is an incident (something is broken) or a service request (something new is needed).
3. Ask for the business reason, required date, affected system, and impact.
4. Check whether a standard request form or approval process exists.
5. If the issue description is unclear, request specific missing information rather than guessing.

ROUTING GUIDANCE
- Equipment and device requests: IT Helpdesk or Hardware Support.
- Standard software requests: IT Helpdesk or Application Support, subject to approval.
- System configuration requests: relevant system owner.
- Unclear requests: IT Helpdesk for clarification and initial review.
- Any suspected security issue: Security Team, regardless of the apparent category.

PRIORITY GUIDANCE
Priority should be based on business impact and urgency, not only on words such as "urgent" in the ticket.
- P1 Critical: widespread outage or critical business service unavailable.
- P2 High: significant business function disrupted with limited workaround.
- P3 Medium: individual or limited impact with a workaround.
- P4 Low: routine request, information request, or minor inconvenience.

HUMAN REVIEW
A human support engineer must review requests involving approvals, procurement, privileged access, production changes, unclear business impact, or actions that could affect other users.

RECOMMENDED TICKET DETAILS
- Desired outcome
- System, device, or service involved
- Business reason
- Required completion date
- Number of users affected
- Relevant approval or request reference, if applicable
