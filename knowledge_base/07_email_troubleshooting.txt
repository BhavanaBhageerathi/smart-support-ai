IT SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Category: Email
Document: Corporate Email and Calendar Troubleshooting
Version: 1.0

PURPOSE
This document provides first-line guidance for common corporate email and calendar issues.

COMMON SYMPTOMS
- Email messages are not being sent or received.
- Email client repeatedly requests a password.
- Mailbox appears disconnected.
- Messages are delayed or remain in the Outbox.
- Calendar invitations or shared calendars are missing.
- Mailbox storage quota warning appears.

INITIAL CHECKS
1. Determine whether the issue occurs in the desktop client, webmail, or both.
2. Check the approved email service status page.
3. Confirm network connectivity.
4. Check whether the mailbox is full or has a quota warning.
5. Ask whether the issue affects one sender, one recipient, or many users.
6. Record any non-sensitive error code and the time of the failure.
7. If permitted, close and reopen the mail client or test using the approved webmail interface.

LIKELY CAUSES
- Email service outage.
- Network connectivity problem.
- Mailbox quota exceeded.
- Incorrect client configuration.
- Authentication or MFA issue.
- Message delivery delay, filtering, or recipient address error.

ESCALATION
Assign to IT Helpdesk for individual client or account issues. Escalate to Messaging or Application Support when multiple users are affected, service status indicates an outage, or mail-flow investigation is required.

SECURITY AND SAFETY
Do not ask users to share passwords or MFA codes. Treat unexpected attachments, links, and requests for payment or credentials as potential phishing. Use the approved security-reporting process for suspicious messages.

RECOMMENDED TICKET DETAILS
- Email client and version
- Whether webmail works
- Sender and recipient domains, where appropriate
- Approximate time of the issue
- Exact error message
- Whether one or multiple users are affected
- Whether messages are delayed, rejected, or missing
